# How to identify real phishing emails:
1. Check the sender's email address.
2. The use of urgent language- Make note of any messages such as "Your account will be closed today".
3. Check the link by hovering- Look at where the link leads to.
4. Spelling errors- Many phishing emails have spelling mistakes.
5. Password request- No legitimate companies will ever ask you for your password via an email.
6. Un expected attachment- If an unexpected attachment then don't open it.
## What to do:
1. Don't click or reply to the email.
2. Don't download the attachment.
3. Report it to IT/Security.
4. Delete the email.
